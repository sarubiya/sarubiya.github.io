jenius Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-22.

jenius is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner (bjahn2k@gmail.com) to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

It uses Google user data (youtube.upload, youtube scopes) only to upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's channel. It does not collect data from any other user. OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's local PC and are never sent to any other server. No data is sold or shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account third-party access settings.

jenius's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
